05 / Outlook Security & Compliance · Case study

Protect the meeting, not just the message.

Bringing enterprise-grade protection and Teams conversation into the Outlook calendar.

  • Enterprise trust
  • Outlook + Teams
  • Cross-platform
  • Shipped
Role
Designer, Outlook Security & Compliance and Calendar
Timeline
2022 – 2023
Team
Outlook, Teams and Microsoft Purview partners across India, Redmond and Vancouver
Platform
Outlook for Windows, web and Mac; Microsoft Teams

The problem

Sensitive meetings needed the same protection as sensitive email, across both Outlook and Teams.

What I led

Sensitivity labels for meetings, clearer label treatments, data-loss prompts and chat from the calendar.

Outcome

Announced at Microsoft Ignite 2022; meeting labels were part of the Teams Premium launch.

Context

Meetings increasingly carry sensitive content across Outlook and Teams: the invite, shared files, chat and recordings. Organizations already labeled sensitive email and documents, but meetings were a gap.

At the same time, the conversation about a meeting lived in Teams, separate from the Outlook calendar where it was scheduled.

What I designed

Compliant meetings

Organizers apply a sensitivity label when scheduling from the Outlook calendar, consistently across Windows, web and Mac. I extended it to delegate scenarios and Classic Outlook for Windows.

Clearer labels

More prominent label treatments and external tags, so people notice sensitive meetings and external participants.

Data-loss prompts

Oversharing prompts at send time, including the wait-on-send and offline-send cases.

Chat from the calendar

Message a meeting’s Teams chat directly from Outlook, without switching apps.

How I led

  • Picked up work that started in Redmond and became the bridge between the India and Redmond teams.
  • Started and drove design conversations with Redmond partners at key points, bringing the teams into alignment.
  • Partnered with Outlook in Redmond and Teams in Vancouver on research and validation, and drove customer studies.
  • Filled product gaps and helped unblock engineering on time-critical security work.

Key decisions

Same label, same meaning, everywhere

Why
A label has to behave the same on every Outlook client and in Teams, or people stop trusting it.
Trade-off
More coordination across platforms and teams.

Visible at the moment it matters

Why
People should never miss that a meeting is sensitive or includes external guests.
Trade-off
Louder visual treatment, balanced so it informs without alarming.

Interrupt only when it matters

Why
Data-loss prompts appear at send time, and handle offline and waiting states gracefully.
Trade-off
More edge cases to design and test.

Keep the conversation with the meeting

Why
Switching apps to talk about a meeting breaks flow.
Trade-off
A dependency across Outlook and Teams to manage.

Outcome

  • Compliant meetings and chat from the calendar were both announced at Microsoft Ignite 2022 and highlighted in the keynote.
  • Sensitivity labels for meetings were part of the Microsoft Teams Premium launch.
  • Chat from the Outlook calendar became available to customers worldwide.

Security features succeed when people barely notice the friction and never miss the signal.
